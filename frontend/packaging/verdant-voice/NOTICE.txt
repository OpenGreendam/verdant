Verdant Voice 0.1.0 ONNX base — attribution

Voice identity: 童声 01 / 楠楠 (VID 10008), identified by OpenGreendam project
maintainers with the built-in girl voice of an early Chinese reading-software
package. That voice is the original source of the derivative known as
「绿坝1号音源」, which 荼荼丸 produced in June 2009 with a +25% pitch shift for
《绿坝绿坝☆河蟹你全家feveR.》; this package uses the unshifted original. The
acoustic reference bank contains data derived from that source. Project ONNX
controllers and decoder are the frozen Verdant R13B/R12B graphs used by the R14
reference strategy.

See the Verdant NOTICE.md for full runtime and research attribution.
https://github.com/OpenGreendam/verdant/blob/main/NOTICE.md
https://github.com/OpenGreendam/docs/blob/main/MODEL-FORMAT-AND-RELEASE.md

The inherited rights.json records the original local-use qualification of the
source bank. AUTHORIZATION.json records the later release-owner statement for
the complete hash-bound runtime package.
